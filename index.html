About

Nero Dewar, also known as Self.LR and James Ritchie, is a record producer and vocalist from South London. His enigmatic profile makes him one of the most diverse and prolific music producers in the world. In 2025, he released 10 albums and over 200 songs. Visit his Spotify for more… The Best of Nero Dewar compilation is out now. It is Nero Dewar's celebration of 12 months of hard work... A note on the album

I came for the destruction of all evil. Not some poxy music career. Nevertheless, ordinary people might just appreciate this album half as much as I enjoyed playing it for you. Every song in this album is deep within my experiences of life, love, and loss. I gave it my all!
